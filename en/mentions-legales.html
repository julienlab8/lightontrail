Personal data

This site has no personal account area and only collects data in two cases:

  • The newsletter: your email address is stored through our sending tool Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, France), solely to send you information about the LIGHT ON events. Every email contains an unsubscribe link.
  • The emails you send us: they are used solely to reply to you.

Race registrations are made on the njuko platform, which has its own privacy policy. The processing of participants' data is detailed in the event rules.

Your data is neither sold nor transferred. In accordance with the GDPR and the French Data Protection Act, you have a right of access, rectification, erasure and objection: simply write to hello@lightontri.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL.

Cookies

This site uses no analytics tool and no advertising cookie. The 2026 edition film is hosted on YouTube and only loads (in privacy-enhanced mode) if you click play. The course maps are provided by OpenRunner.
