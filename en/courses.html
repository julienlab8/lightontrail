17 KM*

The lakeshore, a few honest but reasonable climbs, and all the scenery. Perfect for a first trail or a comeback.

Elevation530 m D+
Start9:30
Price€19
Register for the 17 KM* View the track · OpenRunner Download the GPX

Who's it for?

A first trail, road runners who want a taste of the trails, or anyone after the scenery without spending the whole day on it.

Aid stations

One aid station on the route and the finish aid station at the Village LIGHT ON. Enough to get round without heavy kit.

Cut-off time

Just one, at km 11.3 at 2h15, for everyone's safety. Time confirmed before the race.

37 KM**

Ridgelines, deep forests and technical sections along the lake. It climbs, it drops… and it starts again.

Elevation1250 m D+
Start8:30
Price€39
Register for the 37 KM** View the track · OpenRunner Download the GPX

Who's it for?

For trail regulars who enjoy climbing and technical terrain. Demanding, but entirely doable with regular training.

Aid stations

Two aid stations with drinks and food, at km 11 and km 26, plus the full aid station at the finish at the Village LIGHT ON. Details and kit on the Practical Info page.

Cut-off times

Cut-off at km 11.3 at 2h15 and at km 26.2 at 4h45 (Col de la Chapelotte, on the way back), for everyone's safety. Times confirmed before the race.

54 KM***

The big format on the heights above the lake: ridgelines, deep forests and the biggest chunk of climbing of the day. For those who love long outings… and good aid stations.

Elevation1900 m D+
Start7:30
Price€49
Register for the 54 KM*** View the track · OpenRunner Download the GPX

Who's it for?

For runners comfortable with long outings and technical terrain. It's the toughest of the three: better to arrive with some kilometres in the legs.

Aid stations

Three aid stations with drinks and food on the route, at km 11.3, 28.3 and 43.4, plus the full aid station at the finish at the Village LIGHT ON. Details and mandatory kit on the Practical Info page.

Start & cut-offs

Start at 07:30 from the Village LIGHT ON, the first of the three. Cut-offs at km 11.3 (2h15, i.e. 09:45), km 28.3 (5h30, i.e. 13:00) and km 43.4 (8h30, i.e. 16:00); finish to be reached within 10h30 maximum, before 18:00.
